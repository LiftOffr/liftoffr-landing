## The strategic split

The pivot audit found the audience is **lifestyle-first, not finance-first** (1.3M views → 8 email captures). That diagnosis drives this list:

- **Tier A brands fit the brand.** Crypto/finance tools. Smaller addressable spend, but the LiftOffr positioning is exactly what they want and the content proves it.
- **Tier B brands fit the audience.** Auto/EDC/lifestyle. This is where the 100K–550K car-POV reels actually deliver, and it carries **zero financial-compliance surface** — no disclaimer gymnastics, no advice risk.

Tier B is the higher-probability money on current content. Tier A is the higher-strategic-value money because it compounds with the $29 funnel. Work both; lead with B for cash, A for positioning.

## Cold email template

Subject: `Bitcoin cycle creator — 1.3M views/90d — partnership?`

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm Torin — I run LiftOffr, a Bitcoin cycle-education account. 4M+ lifetime views, 1.3M in the last 90 days, and my top reels land between 100K and 550K.
>
> I publicly called the October 2025 top at $124,824 and every call is timestamped on my site, which is most of why people follow me. I don't do leverage, exchanges, or price predictions — [brand] is one of maybe fifteen companies I'd actually put in a reel.
>
> Specifically I'd do: [one concrete concept — e.g. "the seed-phrase backup flow, shot as a POV, no voiceover, the format that does my 300K+ numbers"].
>
> Rate card attached. Happy to send recent analytics screenshots if useful.
>
> — Torin
> liftoffr.com

**Rules for this template**
- Replace `[one concrete concept]` every single time. That line is the whole email.
- Never open with your follower count if the view count is the stronger number. It is.
- Attach the rate card as a PDF, don't paste it in the body.
- One follow-up after 6 business days. Then stop. A third email costs you the brand.
- If they ask for a media kit, send the rate card one-pager — that *is* the media kit.

## Tracking

Log every send in a simple table: brand, date sent, route, reply (y/n), outcome, rate quoted. Six months from now the pattern in that table is worth more than any single deal — it tells you which category actually pays you.
