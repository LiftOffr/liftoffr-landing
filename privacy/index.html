Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

LiftOffr ("we," "us") is operated by Torin Christianson. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and your choices.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — when you take the cycle-position quiz, sign up for the Sunday Score, or buy a product
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Whop; we never see card numbers
  • Usage analytics — via Google Analytics 4, Vercel Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity (page views, session duration, anonymized location, device type, referrer)
  • Cookies — used by GA4, Clarity, and Whop to remember sessions and attribute conversions

2. How we use it

  • Deliver your quiz result, the weekly Score email, and any paid content you've requested
  • Send transactional emails (welcome, billing confirmations, churn alerts) via Resend
  • Measure which content and channels work, so we improve what we ship
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

3. Who we share it with

Service providers we use to run LiftOffr — each with their own privacy policy:

  • Whop — checkout, purchases, and Discord access
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Microsoft Clarity — usage analytics
  • Vercel — site hosting

We do not sell your data. We do not share email addresses with third parties for marketing.

4. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email — one click in the footer of every message
  • Request deletion of your email address by writing to torin@liftoffr.com
  • Disable analytics cookies via your browser settings or extensions (uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, etc.)
  • Request a refund within 30 days from your Whop account — purchases are one-time, so there is no subscription to cancel

5. Data retention

Email addresses are retained while you're subscribed and for 90 days after unsubscribe (for compliance and re-subscription handling). Analytics data is retained per GA4 default (14 months) and Clarity default (13 months).

6. Children

LiftOffr is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. International users

LiftOffr is operated from the United States. By using the site or signing up, you consent to your data being processed in the US. EU/UK residents have rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability) — exercise them by emailing torin@liftoffr.com.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify subscribers by email.

9. Contact

Questions: torin@liftoffr.com
