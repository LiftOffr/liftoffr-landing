The Indicators We Track

Five names. One confluence verdict.

If you've seen these mentioned by serious on-chain analysts, you know why we use them. If you haven't — here's the plain-English version.

CBBI
Composite of 9 cycle indicators. Above 85 = top territory. Below 15 = bottom.
What you'd do: Above 85, take staged profits. Below 30, lean into your DCA.
Pi Cycle
When the 111-day MA crosses 2× the 350-day MA. Called the top in 2013, 2017, and April 2021.
What you'd do: Tighten stops or take profits within days of the cross.
MVRV-Z
Compares market value to realized value. Above 5 = euphoria. Below 0 = capitulation.
What you'd do: Above 7 = exit zone. Below 0 = generational entry.
Puell Multiple
Miner profitability. Above 4 = miners overpaid (top). Below 0.5 = miners capitulating (bottom).
What you'd do: Above 4 = de-risk. Below 0.5 = aggressive accumulation.
Rainbow
Bitcoin price colored against a logarithmic regression band. Red zone = "maximum bubble territory."
What you'd do: Red band = scale out. Blue/violet = scale in.

No single indicator is the whole story. Pi Cycle called the top early in April 2021. MVRV-Z fired late in 2017. That's why we use them together. Confluence is the system. One signal is a guess.
