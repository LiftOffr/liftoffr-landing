Start free if you are learning what the indicators mean. Look at the Plan preview if you want a focused document and worksheet. Choose the System if you want a structured course. You do not need to buy all three.

LiftOffr provides education and research tools. The Score describes model conditions; it cannot guarantee what price does next. Your budget, decisions and circumstances remain your own.
