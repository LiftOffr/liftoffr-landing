Three questions to answer in writing

  1. What would make me review my plan, other than price? A Score band change, a new low, a date, or a change in my own situation.
  2. How long could this money sit untouched? Past cycles took years to recover their highs. Money you might need sooner is a different decision.
  3. What will I do if this time is different? Write the rule now, while nothing is forcing you to.
What this map can't tell you. Two to three measured cycles is a tiny sample. Timings have varied and may not repeat; the halving's role is debated. The Score is computed over public historical data (a backtest), not a record of trades. Nothing here predicts a price or a date.
