Bitcoin Cycle Trading Guides

Deep dives on the indicators behind Bitcoin cycle timing — what each one measures, where it has been right, and where it has been wrong.

Which of these are actually in the Score, and which aren't. The LiftOffr Score weights nine components: RHODL Ratio 20%, Puell Multiple 20%, Trolololo 15%, MVRV Z-Score 15%, Pi Cycle Top 10%, 2-Year MA Multiplier 5%, Reserve Risk 5%, Woobull Top Cap 5%, RUPL 5%.

Four of the guides below cover a weighted component: Puell, MVRV, Pi Cycle and the 2-Year MA Multiplier. The Rainbow Chart guide covers the logarithmic regression band the Trolololo component is built on. The CBBI, Fear & Greed and Google Trends guides cover indicators that carry no weight in the Score at all — CBBI is the public data source the component readings come from, and the other two are written about here purely as context that people ask about.

And four weighted components have no guide yet — RHODL Ratio, Reserve Risk, Woobull Top Cap and RUPL, which between them carry 35% of the Score. Their live readings, weights and a section on where each has been wrong are at /indicators in the meantime.
