## Why the links are `/r/N` and `/p/N`

Each one 307s to the real page carrying
`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=phase_b&utm_content=N`. That tag now
survives internal navigation and gets appended to the Whop checkout URL, so a sale can be
traced back to the specific reel that produced it. Verified end to end on 20 Aug.

Before today none of that worked, which is why the last 90 days of reach are unreadable and
why nobody can say whether the 60 failed checkouts came from Instagram or Whop Discover.
**Use the `/r/N` links rather than typing `liftoffr.com/score` — a bare link is an untagged
link and the piece becomes unmeasurable.**
