## C. Design research addendum (2026-07-01, redesign question)
**Verdict: NO visual redesign.** Dark near-black + single red accent = exactly the ITC "quant credibility" playbook; winners differ by STRUCTURE not style. Key findings:
- Bio-page structure that wins (cold IG mobile): identity header (face+handle) → promise headline matching the reel hook → email form above fold → magnet visual+bullets → proof strip → ONE secondary trial card → repeat CTA. NO nav, no link menus (1:1 attention ratio; Unbounce: extra links cost 40%+).
- Capture-first beats trial-first for cold bio traffic even with a $0 trial (magnet asks 15-25% vs trial asks 5-8% cold); trial becomes the pitch on thank-you + emails 1-3. Benchmark: tight squeeze pages do 20-30%; our 5% = structure/match failure.
- Mobile fold pattern across winners (BM Pro/Milk Road/ITC): headline + ONE number + ONE CTA — never features first-screen. Milk Road repeats one verbatim price-anchored CTA 7+×; proof (live P&L) is section two.
- Whop-native storefronts convert on verified member/review counts alone → argues for growing real testimonial + member proof (Tier 2).
- APPLIED 2026-07-01: /checklist restructured to this spec (identity header, promise H1, attention leaks cut). Homepage left alone — fold already matches pattern, mid-measurement.
