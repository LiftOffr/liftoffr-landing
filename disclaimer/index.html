4. Past performance, and what “the record” actually is

Every dated signal on this site is the LiftOffr Score computed over public historical price and on-chain data. It is a backtest, not a record of trades placed or calls published at the time. The formula did not exist for most of the period it is measured against. No person and no customer of LiftOffr acted on the dated signals in that log when they printed, because they did not print — they were reconstructed afterwards. Separately, Torin describes laddering out early ahead of the 2025 top on /about, and says being early cost him the last leg. That is his own account of his own conduct, it is deliberately unquantified, and you cannot audit it. Treat it as a personal statement, not as part of the published record and not as evidence the model works.

Backtests are hypothetical. They exclude taxes, exchange fees, slippage, and the behaviour of a real person on a bad day. Past performance — ours, anyone’s, or any indicator’s — does not predict future results.

The complete record is published at /receipts: all 64 zone crossings the Score has produced between 23 September 2011 and 30 November 2025, including every one that went the wrong way. Scored directionally, the model was right about direction on 21 of 46 directional crossings at 30 days, 21 of 46 at 90 days, and 25 of 46 at 180 days. That is close to a coin flip on a short horizon and it is not published as a win rate, an accuracy figure, or a hit rate. It is a multi-month cycle-zone tool and a 30-day directional test is a poor proxy for what it is built to do.
