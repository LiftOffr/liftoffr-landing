## How To Use This Checklist

When **5 or more** of these 8 indicators flash "TOP ZONE" simultaneously, the cycle is statistically near peak. No single indicator is reliable on its own — confluence is the signal.

## ✅ The 8 Indicators

### 1. CBBI (Colin Talks Crypto Bull-Bear Index)
**What it is:** Composite score of 11 on-chain + market indicators, scaled 0–100.
**Trigger zone:** **85+** = top zone. **95+** = historical cycle top.
**Plain English:** The single most reliable cycle gauge. The 2013, 2017, and 2021 tops all hit 95+; the October 2025 top peaked slightly lower at 94 max — still deep in top territory and a clear cycle peak signal.
**Where to check:** colintalkscrypto.com/cbbi/

---

### 2. Pi Cycle Top Indicator
**What it is:** Crossover of the 111-day MA and 2x the 350-day MA.
**Trigger zone:** **Cross within 3% of each other** = warning. **Full cross** = signal fired.
**Plain English:** Called the top within 3 days in 2013, 2017, and April 2021 — but missed the November 2021 ATH and the October 2025 cycle peak ($124,824). Binary signal — when it fires it's deadly; when it doesn't, lean on the other 7 indicators.
**Where to check:** lookintobitcoin.com/charts/pi-cycle-top-indicator/

---

### 3. MVRV Z-Score
**What it is:** Distance between BTC market cap and "realized" cap, normalized.
**Trigger zone:** **5+** = top zone. **7+** = extreme.
**Plain English:** Measures how overvalued BTC is vs. what holders actually paid. The 2013, 2017, and 2021 tops all hit MVRV above 7; the October 2025 top reached top zone with lower raw values, reflecting maturing institutional flows. Still a reliable confluence signal.
**Where to check:** lookintobitcoin.com/charts/mvrv-zscore/

---

### 4. Market Cipher B (Weekly Timeframe)
**What it is:** Custom oscillator combining money-flow, RSI, and divergence detection.
**Trigger zone:** **Weekly bearish divergence** with VWAP rejection.
**Plain English:** When weekly Cipher B prints a red dot at the top of its range with bearish divergence, it's exit territory. Rare and high-conviction.
**Where to check:** TradingView (paid indicator).

---

### 5. BTC Rainbow Chart
**What it is:** Logarithmic regression bands colored from blue (cheap) to red (overvalued).
**Trigger zone:** **Red zone** ("Maximum Bubble Territory") = sell signal.
**Plain English:** A rough visual heuristic. Historically, price entering the top red band has coincided with late-cycle readings — it is a description of where price sits, not an instruction.
**Where to check:** blockchaincenter.net/bitcoin-rainbow-chart/

---

### 6. 2-Year Moving Average Multiplier
**What it is:** BTC price vs. 2-year MA × 5.
**Trigger zone:** **Price above the red line** (5x 2Y MA) = top zone.
**Plain English:** Every previous cycle has topped within months of price crossing this line. Slow-moving but extremely reliable.
**Where to check:** lookintobitcoin.com/charts/2-year-moving-average/

---

### 7. Crypto Fear & Greed Index
**What it is:** Aggregated sentiment score 0–100 based on volatility, momentum, social media.
**Trigger zone:** **90+ ("Extreme Greed")** sustained for 2+ weeks.
**Plain English:** Sentiment indicator only. Useful as confirmation, not a primary signal. Tops happen when everyone is euphoric — this measures euphoria.
**Where to check:** alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/

---

### 8. Google Trends — "Bitcoin"
**What it is:** Worldwide search interest for "Bitcoin," scaled 0–100.
**Trigger zone:** **80+** sustained = retail FOMO peak.
**Plain English:** When your aunt is googling "how to buy Bitcoin," the top is near. Search hit 100/100 at the 2017 and November 2021 peaks — but the October 2025 cycle topped without retail FOMO ever materializing. First cycle to top without this signal firing.
**Where to check:** trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=bitcoin
