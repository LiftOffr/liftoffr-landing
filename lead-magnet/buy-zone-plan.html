The Bear Market Buy Zone Plan

How I decide when the bottom is forming, where I place my buys, and when the window closes — the same framework I'm running with my own money right now. July 2026 edition.

1 · The bottom is a zone, not a price

Nobody nails the exact low — I certainly haven't. In 2022 I sat in my dorm room between two college classes and watched $30K disappear because I had no plan for either direction. What history does repeat: in the 2015, 2018 and 2022 bear markets, Bitcoin's low formed at or below the 200-week moving average. That line is the anchor. Everything below builds on it.

2 · The four buy bands (anchored to the 200W MA)

Instead of one "bottom call," I pre-define four bands as multiples of the 200-week MA. The MA moves — recompute monthly. As of July 2026 the 200W MA sits near $62.5K, so:

BandTrigger (× 200W MA)July 2026 exampleWhat it has meant historically
Band 10.97×~$60,700Price reaches the MA — value territory begins
Band 20.85×~$53,200Typical bear-market undershoot
Band 30.73×~$45,600Deep-value zone of past cycle lows
Band 40.65×~$40,600Capitulation extreme — rare, brief
BTC traded near $61.6K as this was written — already inside Band 1. Deeper bands may never fill. That's the point of the split: you don't need the exact low, you need exposure across the zone.

3 · The capital split

Decide your total accumulation budget for the bear first — money you won't need for 4+ years. Then split it:

BucketAllocationHow it deploys
Baseline weekly DCA50%Every week, no matter what, until the window closes
Band 1 tranche20%Limit order / manual buy at 0.97× MA
Band 2 tranche15%At 0.85× MA
Band 3 tranche10%At 0.73× MA
Band 4 tranche5%At 0.65× MA

The DCA half guarantees you accumulate even if the deeper bands never print. The tranche half guarantees the crash you're afraid of becomes the buying opportunity you planned for.

4 · Confirmation signals (don't buy the falling knife blind)

✓LiftOffr Score below 40 — accumulation zone (below 20 = aggressive). It's at 26 right now. Free live read: liftoffr.com/cycle

✓Fear & Greed under 25 for multiple weeks — sentiment washout

Three or more at once = the zone is open. One alone = wait.

5 · When the window closes

→Score climbs back above 40 and holds for 3+ weeks
→Price reclaims the 200W MA and holds it as support
→Cancel unfilled deeper-band orders, keep the baseline DCA, and switch your attention to the exit framework — that's a different checklist (I have one).

6 · The rules that save you from yourself

1.Write the plan before the crash. Decisions made mid-panic are the ones I've paid $30K to learn about.
2.Limit orders, not willpower. Pre-place the tranche buys. The best fills happen at 3am when you're asleep.
3.No leverage. Ever. The zone can go 20% deeper than any model. Spot survives that; leverage doesn't.
4.Withdraw to a hardware wallet. Bear markets kill exchanges (I lived through NiceHash and FTX). Not your keys, not your coins.
5.4-year money only. If you might need it sooner, it doesn't belong in the plan.
