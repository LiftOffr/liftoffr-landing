## Notes for Torin — what the big campaigns actually do

**Be aware of what I could and couldn't verify.** Brez's and TJR's campaign
pages are public, but their actual rule text sits behind the member area, so I
could not read their literal requirements. Their public pages show the rate and
the pitch and nothing else — Brez is **$2 per 1,000 views**, roughly double
yours, and TJR runs a **$3,300 daily budget** created around 11:30 EST, with the
top 3 clippers each month getting into an "ELITE" server. I'm not going to
invent their rule text.

What **is** well-documented as standard practice across clipping campaigns, and
what I've built into your requirements:

- **Clippers post on their own accounts**, not on a brand account. That's how
  view tracking attributes to them, and it's how Whop pays. Theme/fan pages are
  normal and generally allowed.
- **"Must tag the brand in the caption" is a standard, explicitly shipped
  rule** — it's cited as a typical campaign requirement alongside things like
  "memes not allowed" and "on-screen text must cover at least a third of the
  screen". Requiring the tag is normal and expected, not a big ask.
- **Missing required hashtags or credits is one of the standard rejection
  reasons**, so tying approval to the tag is a conventional enforcement lever.
- A strong brief is expected to specify **"the exact tag and caption required"**
  rather than leaving it to taste. Yours now does.

Two things I'd suggest you decide on, which the research doesn't settle:

1. **IG Collaborator vs plain @mention.** A collab post puts your handle on the
   post itself and pushes it to your followers too — much stronger than a
   caption mention. But every collab needs *you* to accept the invite, which at
   volume becomes a daily chore. I've written it as "add as Collaborator if
   available" so it's encouraged but the caption tag is the hard requirement.
2. **TJR's tiered incentive is worth copying.** A monthly "top 3 clippers get
   X" costs nothing up front and is the single cheapest quality lever in their
   model. You have no equivalent. Something like top-3 get a rate bump to
   $1.50/1K next month would fit your budget.
