# DM scripts — Part 4.6 of the 30-day plan

**Status: reference copy. Nothing here sends, and nothing here should ever be automated.**
Written 2026-08-20 from the plan's Part 4.6. These are for Torin to send by hand.

Lives in `emails/` beside `BUYER_SEQUENCE.md` and `BROADCASTS.md` because it is the
same kind of artifact: drafted copy waiting on a human. `.md` is in `.vercelignore`,
so this never ships to the public site.

---

## Standing rules — every message, no exceptions

- **Never send a price in the first message.**
- **Never** write "guarantee", "profitable", "returns", "you'll make", or "risk-free".
- **Never** give an entry, an exit, a target, or a ticker in a DM. That is the line
  between publishing and advising, and a DM is exactly where it gets crossed — a
  timely, instrument-specific message to one person is outside the publisher's
  exclusion and no disclaimer cures it.
- If someone asks what *they* should do: answer with what **you** do, and point at the
  public record.
- **Do not automate these.** Bulk-sending trading-adjacent DMs is its own problem.

Every link below is live and checkable: `/score` (the free Score),
`/receipts` (all 64 signals), `/indicators` (the nine weights).

## DM-1 — reply to a keyword comment (primary volume script)

> Hey — sending it now.
>
> Here's the free Score page: liftoffr.com/score
> It updates every morning. The nine indicators and their weights are on the same page,
> so you can rebuild the number yourself from free data if you want to check it.
>
> Quick one so I don't send you the wrong thing: have you been through a full cycle
> already, or is this your first one?

*The free thing goes first and unconditionally. The verification invitation is the
hook. The question segments them into the existing quiz taxonomy without making anyone
take a quiz.*

### Branch A — "I've been through one / I rode it up and gave it back"

> Then you already know the part most people learn the expensive way. Mine was 2022 —
> about $30,000, round-tripped, and I had no written sell rule at all.
>
> That's the whole reason the exit ladder exists. It's the four Score levels I sell at
> and the fractions, written down on a boring afternoon rather than on the day it's
> actually happening.
>
> It's in a $29 doc if you want it — but read /receipts first, all 64 signals including
> the ones that went the wrong way. If the record doesn't convince you, the doc won't
> either.

### Branch B — "First cycle / I'm new"

> Then genuinely: don't spend money on any of this yet.
>
> The Score's free, the record's public, and the nine weights are published so you can
> check the number yourself. That's most of the value and it costs nothing. Come back
> when you've got a position big enough that the sell decision actually matters.

*This branch costs sales on purpose. It is what the quiz already does for the `NEW`
segment, and it is the cheapest credibility available.*

### Branch C — "I'm holding and doing nothing"

> That's the most common one, and it's not really a strategy — it's a decision you're
> deferring until the day it's hardest to make.
>
> The free Score tells you where the cycle sits. The bit it doesn't do is decide for
> you in advance. That's what the ladder is: four thresholds, written down while calm.
> $29 doc, 30-day refund.
>
> Either way, /receipts is public. Read it before you spend anything.
