October 2025 — the case that matters

Pi Cycle never fired in 2025.
The Score was in its exit zone anyway
— and stayed there through the top.

This is the argument for weighting nine components instead of trusting one, and it is also the clearest example of what a threshold model costs you. The Score crossed into the exit zone on 27 June 2025 at $107,091, BTC peaked at $124,824 on 6 October, and the Score did not leave the exit zone until 21 October — fifteen days after the peak. It was in the right zone. It was not a clean exit.

Indicator readings at or within one week of each cycle top. Every threshold is independently documented and publicly verifiable.

Two things to hold while you read these. The “confluence at N of 6” counts in the timelines are a legacy heuristic — a hand count of how many of these six indicators were in their own top zones. That is not the LiftOffr Score, which is a weighted average of nine components with published weights and a single 0–100 output. Where the two disagree, the Score is the product and the count is background. And the readings themselves are approximations that vary by data source and by which day you call “the top”; every value here now matches /indicator-history, which is the page that owns them.

What the Score itself did: it crossed into the exit zone on 27 Jun 2025 at $107,091 and was still above the 85 threshold when Bitcoin peaked at $124,824 on 6 October. It left the zone on 21 Oct 2025 at $108,700 — fifteen days after the peak. At 180 days that June crossing was −18.2%.

Pi Cycle, a 10% component, never crossed in this cycle; MVRV, Puell, RUPL, 2YMA and Reserve Risk were all elevated, and the weighted number stayed in the exit band regardless. That is a claim about the Score being robust to one component going quiet. It is not a claim that it called the top. Fear & Greed and Google Trends are cited on this page as context only — both carry zero weight, so neither could have failed the model.
Why this matters: a cycle can top without retail euphoria, so anyone reading one price signal (Pi Cycle) or one sentiment gauge would have had nothing to act on. A weighted composite still produced a number. What it did not produce was a date — it entered the exit zone three times between November 2024 and October 2025 and dropped back out three times — six transitions in all, and the ladder exists because of that, not in spite of it. The November 2024 crossing was followed by six more months of upside. The two 2021 crossings in October and November are the ones that look like the marketing.

A drawdown-avoided figure also assumes you sold everything at the crossing and never bought back, which is not what the plan does and not what I did.
