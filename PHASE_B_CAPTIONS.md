## The rules these were written to

- **The hook never contains the offer.** Roughly 97.5% of views are non-followers who
  never read the caption; the hook has to earn the watch on its own.
- **The offer lives in the caption and the pinned comment.** Never in the hook.
- **Every link is `liftoffr.com/score`**, which is live and is the free tier — live
  number, all nine weights, recompute instructions, all 64 signals. Not `/ig`, not a
  checklist that no longer exists.
- **Zero lifestyle. No cars.** Not one.
- **Compliance, non-negotiable:** no "guarantee", "profitable", "returns", "you'll
  make", "risk-free". No price target, entry, exit, or ticker. No "should". Describe
  what the number *has historically meant*, or what *I* do — never what the viewer
  ought to do.

**Standing pinned comment** (the plan's wording, use on every piece unless a specific
one is given):

> free score + the full 64-signal record, losers included: liftoffr.com/score

## One flag on the plan

Hook 3 says *"wrong 29 times out of 64."* That figure is the count of crossings followed
by an adverse move, and the repo is explicit that it **is not a hit rate and must not be
relabelled as one** — the 64 mix opposing signal types. The caption above uses the
directional scoring (21 of 46 at 30 days) instead, which is the number `/receipts`
publishes. If the hook is spoken verbatim, the caption should carry the correct framing,
which it does. Better still, say "wrong plenty of times and every one is published."
