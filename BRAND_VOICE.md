# BRAND_VOICE.md — write every LiftOffr sentence against this
STATUS: v1, 2026-07-01. Applies to: site, course, Discord, emails, IG captions. Exception at bottom.

## Tone rules
1. First person "I" — Torin talking to one person, never "we" corporate voice.
2. Data + receipts, zero hype. Every claim is a number, a date, or a verifiable link (/track-record, /cycle).
3. Anti-influencer positioning: we mock hopium, leverage, and lambo-promises — we never use them as promises.
4. Story beats open big sections: 2017 mining rig → watching $30K disappear between two college classes (2022) → clean DCA exit at the Oct 6, 2025 top. Canonical phrasing: "between two college classes," never "dorm-room day."
5. Education framing always: "historically X happened," never "you should buy/sell." End market content with "Not financial advice."
6. Short sentences. Fragments fine. Read it aloud — if you wouldn't say it to a friend, cut it.

## Words/patterns — BANNED
"moreover" · "furthermore" · "delve" · "it's important to note" · "in today's fast-paced" · "whether you're a beginner or" · "unlock/unleash your potential" · "game-changer" · "to the moon" · guaranteed anything · price predictions stated as certainty · fake urgency (timers, fake stock counts)

## Words/patterns — USE
"here's the actual number" · "the indicators don't argue" · "confluence" · "the read" · specific dollar amounts and dates · "no card required" · "cancel in one click" · cost-of-inaction urgency ("one mistimed exit costs more than years of membership")

## Before/after examples
1. ❌ "Our comprehensive suite of indicators empowers investors to navigate market cycles."
   ✅ "8 indicators. One daily brief. Every cycle top called since 2013."
2. ❌ "It's important to note that no single indicator is foolproof, so a diversified analytical approach is crucial."
   ✅ "No single indicator is reliable on its own. Confluence is the signal — when 5+ flash top zone together, the cycle is statistically near peak."
3. ❌ "Join our vibrant community of like-minded crypto enthusiasts today!"
   ✅ "58 people checking the same 8 indicators every morning. Come read today's brief — first 7 days are free."

## The one exception
Single-slide IG "banger" proof drops get cocky, Tate-coded edge (asymmetry flex: "i deployed their yearly salary in 8 days"). That energy stays OFF the site, course, emails, and Discord.
