Pricing assumes a hit-range reel (100K–550K views). At $1,500 on 150K views that is a $10 CPM — under the finance-niche market rate, which is where a first rate card should sit.

| **Exclusivity (category)** | **+30% of base** | I don't take a competitor in your category for 60 days from post date. |

**Standard terms**
- 50% on signature, 50% within 7 days of posting. Net 15.
- Content stays up minimum 12 months.
- I keep final edit control on anything in my voice. I will not read a script that makes a claim I can't back.
- One revision round included on dedicated work; further rounds $250 each.
- Turnaround: 2–3 weeks from signed brief. I'm active-duty and film on weekends — I don't take rush work.
- Every paid post is disclosed: `#ad` or `Paid partnership` label, per FTC 16 CFR Part 255. Non-negotiable, and not a thing I'll bury in a caption.

## What I will not take (brand filter — master plan §9 boundary)

Say this out loud in the first email. It is the reason a serious brand pays a premium here.

- ❌ Leveraged/margin/futures products, prop-firm funding offers, or anything sold on "turn $500 into $5,000"
- ❌ Centralized exchanges (custody-first brand; I tell people to hold their own keys)
- ❌ Presales, memecoins, new tokens, NFT mints, "early access" allocations
- ❌ Signal groups, copy-trading, bots sold on promised returns
- ❌ Anything requiring me to state or imply a price prediction or a guaranteed return
- ❌ Gambling, sports betting, prediction markets marketed as investing

**Yes to:** hardware wallets and self-custody tools, Bitcoin-only savings/DCA apps, crypto tax software, charting and on-chain data platforms, security/privacy tools, and non-finance lifestyle brands that fit the actual content (see the auto/EDC tier in `BRAND_DEAL_TARGETS.md`).

## Compliance notes (read before signing anything)

1. **Disclosure is mine to control.** Label goes in the platform's native disclosure tool AND the first line of the caption. If a brand asks me to soften it, that's a decline.
2. **No performance claims about the brand's product.** I can show what a hardware wallet does. I cannot say it will make anyone money.
3. **Nothing that reads as personalized financial advice.** Brand copy gets the same treatment as site copy — education framing, "not financial advice," no "you should buy."
4. **Securities-attorney consult triggers at the first ~$2K revenue month** (master plan constraint floor). Brand-deal income counts toward that trigger.
5. **Affiliate links are disclosed as affiliate links** — separate from #ad, both stated.
6. Keep every signed brief and invoice. This is 1099 income against the EIN.

## Pricing discipline

- **Do not discount below $1,200** for a single reel. A first "portfolio-building" freebie sets your floor at zero and brands talk to each other.
- **Trade product for content only when the product is the content** — a hardware wallet you'd film anyway. Even then, cap it: product + $750, not product alone.
- **Raise the card when a reel clears 300K twice in a quarter.** Re-quote at $2,200 / $3,500 / $4,800.
- If a brand's first counter is under half the card, the fit is wrong. Decline warmly, keep the door open.
