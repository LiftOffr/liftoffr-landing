# Lesson 10 — What carries zero weight, and why

Nine components. Plenty of well-known Bitcoin indicators are not among them, and the omissions are deliberate. An indicator can be **context** — worth looking at — without being an **input** the number is built from. Confusing the two is how people end up unable to reproduce a figure they were told they could check.

**CBBI — the source, not a component.** It carries zero weight because it is the *pipe*: the Score reads its nine component values **from** CBBI's public data, so including CBBI's own composite would count the same readings twice. Use it to check my arithmetic — <#1540323997940711494>. If it and the Score diverge, that is two weightings of an overlapping set; the question is which components explain the gap.

**Fear & Greed — sentiment, not an input.** It measures how people feel; the Score measures what the chain and price structure are doing. Sentiment is fast and mean-reverting over days; the Score reads cycle position over months. **At the October 2025 top it never reached Extreme Greed** — anyone requiring it waited for a confirmation that never came. Colour on a reading you already have, never the reading.

**Google Trends — attention, not an input.** Retail attention *follows* price rather than leading it, and its scaling is relative to the window you pick, so there is no stable level to weight. **At the October 2025 top it sat well below 2021 levels.** That cycle topped without the retail spike.

**Proprietary charting tools — not reproducible, so not eligible.** Every component reads from a free public source, so anyone can recompute and check the number. One you cannot independently pull breaks that promise and puts you back to trusting me. They also fire on hourly and four-hourly timeframes — a different question from a multi-month cycle reading.

**Nine public, weighted, reproducible measurements. Nothing else feeds the number.** Not sentiment, not search interest, not a composite of its own inputs, and nothing you have to pay a third party to see.
