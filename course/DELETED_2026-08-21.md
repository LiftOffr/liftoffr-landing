# Deleted course channels — 21 August 2026

**Torin's instruction: delete the old course material outright, not archive it.** This reverses
the keep-with-a-notice approach taken on 20–21 August. His reasoning, and it is right: a server
carrying both a live and a retired version of the same lesson is the two-versions problem this
whole engagement exists to remove. Nobody was mid-course, so nothing was taken from anyone.

The channels are gone. This file is the audit trail, so the decision stays checkable even though
the content does not.

**Every one of these was read in full before deletion**, through the Discord web client — the bot
API returns empty `content` for app-posted embeds, which is how this material went unexamined
through three audits. Nothing was deleted sight-unseen.

## What was deliberately NOT carried across

**Pi Cycle's structural failure hypotheses** — that ETF and institutional flows might dampen the
parabolic 111-day MA rise, that cycles could elongate, that near-misses exist. Honest hedging, but
speculation about *why* it might fail in future rather than a record of where it has failed.
`#m4-l5-pi-cycle-top-10pct` says instead that it did not cross at the 2025 top, that three hits
and a miss is a small sample, and that a signal firing only at the very top tells you nothing for
the other 1,400 days of a cycle. That is evidence rather than hypothesis, and it is the stronger
version. Recorded here so the omission is a decision rather than an oversight.

**Module 3 lesson 5 on leverage was not touched and is not deleted.** It argues the right amount
of leverage is zero for 95% of readers and gives harm-reduction guardrails for the rest. It is the
best lesson in the original course, it depends on no part of the retired framework, and it stays
exactly where it is.
